Privacy Policy

Calendar Ai is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account.

  • It accesses Google Calendar events only to read and manage the owner's own calendar.
  • It does not collect data from any other person.
  • It does not sell or share any data with third parties.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's private server.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
